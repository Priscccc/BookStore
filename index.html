Featured Books


Game of Thrones

By George R. R. Martin

A sweeping epic of dynastic fantasy, where noble families fight for control of the Iron Throne of Westeros.

To the south, the king's powers are failing—his most trusted adviser dead under mysterious circumstances and his enemies emerging from the shadows of the throne

Mockingjay (The Hunger Games)

By Suzanne Collins

The thrilling conclusion to the trilogy, where Katniss Everdeen leads the ultimate rebellion against the Capitol.

Katniss Everdeen is the reluctant face of the Panem rebellion. Driven by survival instincts and specialized combat training, her ultimate objective is to destabilize a tyrannical regime, protect her loved ones, and eventually transition into post-war rehabilitation and family life

Dune

By Frank Herbert

A stunning blend of adventure and mysticism, environmentalism and politics on the desert planet Arrakis.

Dune follows young Paul Atreides as his noble family takes control of the harsh desert planet Arrakis, the universe's only source of "spice"—a priceless resource vital for space travel.

The Hobbit

By J.R.R. Tolkien

A timeless classic where Bilbo Baggins is whisked away on an epic quest to reclaim the Lonely Mountain from a dragon.

The Hobbit is set in Middle-earth and follows home-loving Bilbo Baggins, the titular hobbit who joins the wizard Gandalf and the thirteen dwarves of Thorin's Company on a quest to reclaim the dwarves' home and treasure from the dragon Smaug.

The Little Prince

By Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

A beautiful, poetic tale of a pilot stranded in the desert who meets a young prince fallen from a tiny asteroid.

The narrator, an airplane pilot, crashes in the Sahara desert. The crash badly damages his airplane and leaves the narrator with very little food or water.
